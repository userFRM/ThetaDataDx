Two things going on, and neither is FPSS.

**1. The hang is MDDS, not FPSS.** `/v2/hist` and `/v2/bulk_hist` are served by MDDS (historical). FPSS is the live streaming feed only, so its CONNECTED status is a red herring. The `handleInvoluntaryDisconnect` loop firing while status reads CONNECTED is a half-open MDDS connection your multi-day 1s backfill wedged: requests queue on the dead socket and time out at 0 bytes. `UND_ERR_HEADERS_TIMEOUT` is your HTTP client giving up on upstream headers that never arrive because the connection is stuck. A terminal restart won't clear it if the stuck session is server-side. Confirm and we'll force-reset your MDDS session on our end.

**2. HTTP 570 "request too large" is expected, not degradation.** A full-strike 1s day is past the per-response cap. That's request sizing, not a feed problem. Chunk by strike range or a shorter window.

To prove MDDS is healthy and skip the wedged terminal channel, run it through the SDK directly (gRPC straight to MDDS, not the REST terminal). It uses a 300s deadline and treats a wedged transport as retryable, so it resets and retries instead of hanging half-open. I reproduced all three of your calls against MDDS just now: your 1-row EOD came back in 0.3s, and the full 1s option chain streamed 41,602 rows for a one-minute window with no 570.

```python
# pip install thetadatadx-py   (import name is thetadatadx)
from thetadatadx import Client
c = Client(api_key="YOUR_KEY")          # historical opens; streaming is NOT started
md = c.market_data

# your hung 1-row EOD  ->  returns in ~0.3s
eod = md.index_history_eod("SPX", "20230616", "20230616")

# your bulk 1s option quote. NOTE: intraday SPX option quotes are root SPXW,
# not SPX (SPX is AM-settled monthly only) -- your root=SPX returns no data.
# strike='*' by default = the whole chain, streamed in chunks (no HTTP 570):
q = md.option_history_quote("SPXW", "20230616", date="20230616", interval="1s")

# the fix for the 570 on full days: bound the chain instead of pulling every strike
q = md.option_history_quote("SPXW", "20230616", date="20230616",
                            interval="1s", strike_range=15)
```

A full 0DTE chain at 1s for a whole day is millions of rows either way. Bound it with `strike_range` (or loop day by day) and it streams cleanly. The SDK won't 570 and won't wedge like the raw REST terminal did.
